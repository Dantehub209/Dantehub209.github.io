About

I'm a computer science graduate working in IT services for a courier and logistics business. I like problems where the software has to match how people actually work on the ground — drivers in the field, finance teams approving payments, managers who need one honest number.

I work across the stack: React and TypeScript on the front end, Python and FastAPI on the back end, and Postgres (via Supabase) underneath. I care about clear audit trails, role-based access and interfaces people can use without training.
